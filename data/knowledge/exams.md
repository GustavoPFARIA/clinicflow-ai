# Lab Exams and Preparation

## Blood test fasting
For a complete blood count (CBC) no fasting is needed. For glucose, lipid panel (cholesterol and triglycerides) and insulin, fast for 8 to 12 hours before collection. Drinking water is allowed during the fast.

## Urine test preparation
Collect the first urine of the morning in the sterile container provided by the lab. Wash the genital area with water only and discard the first stream before collecting the midstream urine.

## Abdominal ultrasound preparation
Fast for 6 hours before an abdominal ultrasound. For a pelvic ultrasound, drink 4 to 6 glasses of water one hour before the exam and do not urinate until after the exam.

## When results are ready
Most blood test results are released within 1 to 2 business days; urine cultures take up to 5 business days. As soon as a result is released by the lab, you receive a WhatsApp message and can ask the assistant to see it.

## How results are delivered securely
Results are only shared with the phone number registered in your patient record. The assistant delivers a short summary; the full report is available at the front desk or with your doctor. Always review results with the physician who requested the exam.

## Electrocardiogram preparation
No fasting is needed for an electrocardiogram (ECG). Avoid body lotion on the chest on the day of the exam and wear comfortable clothing.
