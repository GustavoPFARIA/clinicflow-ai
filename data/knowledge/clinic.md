# ClinicFlow Demo Clinic: General Information

## Opening hours
The clinic is open Monday to Friday from 7:00 to 19:00 and on Saturdays from 7:00 to 12:00. We are closed on Sundays and national holidays. The lab collection room opens at 6:30 on weekdays for fasting exams.

## Location and parking
We are located at Avenida T-63, 1200, Setor Bueno, Goiânia - GO. Free parking is available for patients in the building garage; validate your ticket at the front desk.

## Specialties
We offer Cardiology, Dermatology, General Practice and Gynecology consultations, plus an in-house clinical laboratory.

## Consultation prices
A private consultation costs R$ 250.00 for any specialty. Payment is made online through a secure Stripe link sent on WhatsApp, and the booking is confirmed once payment is received. We accept credit cards and Pix.

## Health insurance
We accept Unimed, Bradesco Saúde, SulAmérica and Amil. Bring your insurance card and an ID document to the appointment. Insured patients do not pay the consultation fee online.

## Cancellation and rescheduling policy
You can cancel or reschedule for free up to 24 hours before the appointment, directly on WhatsApp. Paid appointments cancelled within the free window are refunded to the original payment method in up to 7 business days. Late cancellations and no-shows are not refunded.

## What to bring
Bring an ID document, your insurance card (if any), previous exams related to the consultation and a list of the medications you currently take.

## Talking to a person
Our reception team answers on this same WhatsApp number Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00. Just ask to speak with a person.
